Rebellious Adventure

About rebture

rebture was born from the belief that used material isn't the end of the story — it's the start of something new. Uncompromisingly functional, and still stylish.

Our philosophy

Climbing ropes get retired for safety reasons long before the material itself is done — it's still strong, rugged fabric. rebture gives it a second life as bags and carriers built for everyday use, just as much as for the outdoors. Rebellion against a throwaway mindset, adventure as an attitude.

Who we are

rebture is made by Nina and Flo. Nina designs and sews every piece by hand, Flo takes care of materials, organization, and everything around it. Right now we're testing our current design with friends before moving into a larger batch.

The materials

At the core is recycled climbing rope, complemented by other rescued materials. Every piece carries its own story in texture and color gradient — no two pieces are exactly alike.

Design principle: maximum functionality

Our pieces are built to grow with you: a compact belly bag for the quick trip that unfolds in seconds into a large, functional carrier. One piece, many situations — stylish and versatile.
